Butty privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Applies to Butty version 1.

Butty is a UK canal navigation app made by Flynn Davies. Contact: fdavies.freelance@gmail.com.

The short version

Butty does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

Your location

If you allow it, Butty uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map, follow your boat during a journey and work out how far you have to go. Your location is processed only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not keep a record of where you have been.

You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings. Route planning still works without it.

Map tiles

To draw the map, Butty downloads map images ("tiles") from the providers you can choose between in the app: OpenStreetMap, Esri (satellite imagery) and OpenTopoMap (terrain). Like any website, these providers receive your phone's IP address and the area of map being requested, so they can send the images back. Butty does not send them anything else. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle this.

Settings stored on your phone

Your boat profile (name, length, beam, speed, lock pace), recent searches and display preferences are saved on your phone so the app remembers them. They never leave your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Bug reports

The "Report a bug" button opens your own email app with a pre-filled message that includes your boat profile settings, so we can reproduce the problem. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. If you do, we use it only to fix the problem and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

Butty is not aimed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that involve personal information (such as accounts or saved trips), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app.

Your rights

Because Butty holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions, or if you have emailed us a bug report and want it deleted, contact fdavies.freelance@gmail.com. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
